**Last updated:** 2026-11-12

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is processed when using the SynelsGymTimer app for iPhone and Apple Watch, as well as when visiting this website.

## 2. Overview

SynelsGymTimer is a workout interval timer for iPhone and Apple Watch. The app does not require an account or registration.

SynelsGymTimer does not contain advertisements, third-party advertising SDKs, or analytics SDKs.

Workout settings and health data are not transmitted to servers operated by the developer. The developer does not have access to your health data through the app's normal workout functions.

Certain additional data may be processed when participating in beta testing through Apple TestFlight, contacting the developer, or visiting this website. These activities are explained below.

## 3. Workout Settings and Local Storage

SynelsGymTimer stores your workout settings locally on your device.

These settings may include:

- Number of exercises and sets
- Exercise duration
- Rest periods
- Transition times between exercises or equipment

The app also stores technical information needed to exchange settings between your iPhone and Apple Watch, including randomly generated identifiers, version information, and delivery acknowledgements.

This information helps identify transfers, confirm successful delivery, and prevent outdated settings from being applied again. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

The processing is necessary to provide the app's requested functionality, based on Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), where applicable.

Data remains in the app's local storage until it is overwritten or the corresponding app data is removed.

Device backups and restoration may be handled separately according to your Apple device and iCloud settings.

## 4. Communication Between iPhone and Apple Watch

SynelsGymTimer uses Apple's WatchConnectivity framework to transfer workout settings between your iPhone and Apple Watch when you use the corresponding transfer function.

If the receiving device is temporarily unavailable, a pending transfer may be delivered automatically when the devices reconnect.

Technical information described in Section 3 may also be exchanged to identify and acknowledge transfers.

Health data and previously recorded workout sessions are not transferred through this settings synchronization feature.

The processing is necessary to provide the requested functionality, based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, where applicable.

## 5. Apple Health and HealthKit

The Apple Watch app uses Apple's HealthKit framework to start and manage strength training sessions.

HealthKit also allows an active workout session to support the timer's operation while the app is running in the background.

When a workout ends normally, the app saves a workout record to Apple Health.

The recorded workout information includes, in particular:

- Workout type
- Start time
- End time
- Workout duration

The Apple Watch app requests permission to write workout data to Apple Health. It does not request permission to read previously stored health data.

Without the required HealthKit authorization, workouts cannot be started in the Apple Watch app. The iPhone timer can be used independently.

HealthKit authorization is requested through Apple's system permission interface.

Where the processing of health data is based on consent, the relevant legal provisions are Article 6(1)(a) and Article 9(2)(a) GDPR. HealthKit authorization provides technical permission to access Apple Health and does not, by itself, necessarily constitute explicit consent under the GDPR.

You can revoke HealthKit authorization at any time through the Health or privacy settings on your Apple devices.

Revoking authorization affects future access. Workout records already saved to Apple Health remain there until you delete them. Uninstalling SynelsGymTimer does not automatically delete those records.

Synchronization of health data between Apple devices or through iCloud depends on your Apple settings and is governed by Apple's applicable privacy terms.

For more information, see:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 6. Notifications and Sounds

With your permission, the iPhone app may schedule local notifications to inform you about workout phases.

These notifications are generated and scheduled on your device. They do not require a push notification server operated by the developer.

You can disable notifications at any time in your iPhone settings.

SynelsGymTimer does not use notifications for advertising or tracking.

## 7. Beta Testing Through Apple TestFlight

If you use a beta version of SynelsGymTimer through Apple TestFlight, Apple may process information related to the beta testing process.

Depending on Apple's settings and the type of feedback provided, the developer may receive:

- Crash reports and diagnostic information
- Device and operating system information
- App version and usage information
- Feedback submitted through TestFlight
- Screenshots or other attachments you choose to provide
- Your name or email address, where made available through the testing process or voluntarily included in feedback

For public TestFlight invitations, Apple generally does not disclose your name or email address to the developer unless you provide that information yourself.

The developer uses this information exclusively to evaluate feedback, investigate problems, and improve the app's functionality and stability.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is to conduct beta testing, identify technical issues, and improve the app.

Feedback and diagnostic information stored separately by the developer is deleted when it is no longer needed for these purposes, unless legal retention obligations apply.

Apple may retain beta testing information according to its own retention policies.

Please avoid including sensitive health information or other confidential personal information in feedback unless it is necessary to describe a problem.

For more information about TestFlight data processing, see:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/
